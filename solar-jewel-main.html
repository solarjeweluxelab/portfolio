SolarJewel

Elevating from implementation to distribution

A Seven Year Journey

SolarJewel is a high-precision astronomical instrument that I created from years of devoted study in UX/UI, native development, graphics, breath, meditation and astrology.

I intend for it to solve a quiet, essential need: the ability to remain present in the physical earth plane while staying aligned with the planetary flow. So if one is holding space in ceremony, in their daily flow, or just finding stillness, I want SolarJewel to opens up a portal to the heavens, right on our wrist.

After 7 years of playing in Swift, Kotlin, pushing pixels in Adobe and Figma then a long spiritual break it all came together very suddenly, crystal clear and sparkling right in front of me, like a jewel.

Engineering the User Experience

I have always wanted to be a UX Engineer and spent years training in it and my dream has finally come true. This is both a tech jewel for the soul, but also a professional astrology engine.

Tech Jewelry for the Soul

My aim was to achieve lightweight beauty but with the deep and uncompromising functionality of a professional astrology engine. And wrapped in a presentation layer that feels safe, clear, and highly focused during delicate ceremonial workspace integration tasks.

It has been great when playing music, swimming or sleeping to be able to instantly glance at my chart (or any chart) without my phone. Just on the fly, sometimes I want to be reminded of a placement or transit while brushing my teeth or cleaning the house.

User Experience Engineering

I was determined to make the interface as intuitive as possible, though instructions can still be found in the menu if ever needed.

A discrete secret for professionals: You can compare ancient and contemporary geometric spatial framing systems (including Placidus, Porphyry, and several alternate calculation frameworks) dynamically with a responsive horizontal wipe shortcut layout.

How it works

Use simple swips to shift smoothly through different time periods in order to revisit the precise planetary physics that were informing major foundational life events, or project parameters forward to discover how the geometric landscape of the "now" continues to chart your unique evolutionary path.

Luminous Time Travel

In Transit, Secondary Progression and Solar Arc mode, travel backwards or forwards through time and long press to reset to now. This functionality on both WearOS and WatchOS can also be found on iOS and Android phones and tablets.

Sacred Privacy, Scientific Truth and Sanctuary

In this tool, your information never leaves your wrist boundary; every element of the astronomical mathematical engine runs natively and entirely offline on your local device hardware. Your jewel belongs entirely to you.

Quiet Companion

It is intended to serve as a quiet companion that provides glanceable enchantment while keeping our hands free for the work of living. So a dedicated partner for those who seek to walk between worlds.
